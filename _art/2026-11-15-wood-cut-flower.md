While travelling around the desolation of the desert I was inspired by the vibrance of a flower that inspired the wood cut flowers.  Hand cut from one 1/4 plywood, this concentric ring wood cut brings depth of field to a desert flower.  Hand painted and hand worked these flower centric pieces stand off the wall.  Focusing on the bloom of such stark contrast to the desert landscape.
