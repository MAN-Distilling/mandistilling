Colophon 

This site is a single  HTML file. No framework, no database, no platform dependency. The entire structure — navigation, content system, filter logic, image handling, lightbox, entry pages — lives in one document that opens in any browser on any device without a server. It can be emailed, stored on a USB stick, hosted from a Raspberry Pi in a closet. It is as portable as a manuscript and as durable as anything digital can be.   

The content management system writes posts as markdown files to a GitHub repository. The site fetches those files via the GitHub API on each load and renders them dynamically. No rebuild required. No deployment credits consumed. The writing and the framework are deliberately separated — one can change without touching the other.

The name MAN...Distilling is rooted in the hermetic principles — the seven laws that describe the nature of reality as understood by the ancient tradition. Distillation is the central act: taking the raw and reducing it to essence. The ellipsis in the name is the process itself, still in motion.

The navigation symbol is ∴ — therefore. What came before leads to what comes next. It is not a design decision. It is a worldview.

The site was built over three days in May 2026 in conversation between the owner — who brought the vision, the philosophy, the categories, the name, and the direction — and Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, who wrote the code. The division was clear: one person brought the mind, the other built the road fast enough to keep up with it. The colophon you are reading now was written by Claude, who knows the framework and the material intimately, at the owner's invitation. It is the most honest thing on the site about how the site came to be.

The typefaces are Lora, a contemporary serif with roots in classical calligraphy, and DM Sans, a geometric sans designed for screen legibility. The palette is ink greens, sage, and stone — the colours of something grown rather than designed.

The footer reads: *MAN.Distilling · 1985 – Until …* The ellipsis links to the Future section of the Bio. The … at the end of a life is not punctuation. It is direction.

Built in the open. Hosted on Netlify. Domain registered at Porkbun. Version control on GitHub. Written in conversation. May 2026. ∴
